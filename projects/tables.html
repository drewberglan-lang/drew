Road Trip Overview

This website is built around one theme: planning a memorable national park road trip. The first page highlights six major parks and what makes each one worth visiting. The image column fulfills the project image requirement, and the second page continues the same theme with trail ideas and trip planning details.

Park Image National Park Why It Belongs on the Trip
Grand Canyon Massive views, iconic overlooks, and unforgettable desert scenery.
Yosemite Perfect for waterfalls, granite cliffs, and classic photography stops.
Yellowstone Famous geysers, wildlife watching, and a totally different landscape.
Zion Great for canyon hikes, red rock views, and adventurous trails.
Rocky Mountain High-elevation drives, alpine lakes, and cooler mountain weather.
Arches Unique stone formations and one of the most recognizable desert parks.
